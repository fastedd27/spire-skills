## Diagnosticity question triage

**Falsification: track post-ratification parks traced to a discarded low-diagnosticity question — disproportionate tracing to cut questions means the scoring is mis-calibrated, not unlucky.**

## Two-tier brief structure (the Table-A park audit)

**Falsification: any Table-A-sourced mid-run park is a decision-grade signal to retune the risk rubric — never a signal to shrink Table A further (the Table-A park audit).**

## Typed-consequence answers, Table B only

**Falsification: sample 10% of ratified briefs monthly; if typed-consequence fields cluster into 3–4 templated phrasings across unrelated jobs, the field has decayed to theater — >30% template-clustering means redesign the field, not tune it.**

## Why-clause, Table B only

**Falsification: in park post-mortems, check whether the why-clause would have correctly predicted park/no-park versus raw literal-answer match — >95% agreement between why-clause and literal match means it is decorative, drop it.**
